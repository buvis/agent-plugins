# Anti-pattern detection

Loaded at Tier 2 and Tier 3.

- [SR-RVD-068] Flag each of these fifteen patterns explicitly when it appears, citing the line: a vague qualifier without a number ("highly available", "scalable", "secure", "fast", "robust", "performant"); a technology name dropped (a queue, an orchestrator, a protocol, a blockchain, an AI) without a trade-off justification; a load-bearing concern deferred ("we will add observability later", "we will harden security later", "we will write tests later"); reasoning by analogy ("a large company does it this way") without proving applicability to this scale and context; a diagram without a legend, or boxes the prose does not define; implicit assumptions about the latency, availability, or correctness of dependencies; a single-paragraph section where a critical concern was waved away; a decision justified by team preference rather than a constraint, evidence, or a requirement; a missing failure-mode story for a dependency call; "single source of truth" claimed but not designed (the same state in several stores); performance numbers without a test methodology or environment; "stateless" claimed for a system that holds state (caches, sessions, memory); buzzwords stacked without a delivered system that proves the combination works; estimates in suspiciously round numbers with no decomposition; disproportionate section depth, one section many times the median weight of the others, or a critical section a single paragraph beside a peripheral one of many pages.
