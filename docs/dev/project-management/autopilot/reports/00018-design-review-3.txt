## Prior blockers

**Rotation loses replay credentials: fixed.**
- Receipts keep the authorizing digest and the original response.
- The recorded request can be retrieved with the historical credential, or with the current credential of the same actor and role at an epoch no earlier than the authorizing epoch.
- I traced a lost acknowledgement for `owner.acquire`: the caller reopens the in-place operator path, which now holds epoch N+1, and gets the receipt.
- I traced `owner.release`: the owner descriptor is gone, but the historical digest in the receipt still matches.
- A residual question remains (Q1 below).

**Git can stage plaintext secrets: fixed.**
- `Interfaces & contracts > Kernel` now creates the `*` ignore files before any credential exists.
- The `*` file ignores itself.
- A deeper `.gitignore` outranks parent and global rules.
- Only `git add -f` bypasses it, which is acceptable under the stated threat model: "prevent accidental cross-role use".
- A side effect remains (NB4 below).

**Capacity has no recovery exit: fixed by count, not by bytes.**
- `Kernel > receipts` reserves slots 4095 and 4096 for `owner.release` and `owner.recover`.
- Those two can clear an owner but never acquire one.
- A sealed workflow still allows receipt retrieval and snapshot.
- The ordered archive, hash-verify, rename and re-open procedure is coherent, and the archive rename is guarded by the ancestor inode recheck.
- The byte side is open (NB1 below).

## Checked correct, by interface

**StateStore** (`Kernel`, pinned `store.py`)
- The design keeps single-link regular-file checks, bounded reads, flock CAS, fsync before replace, create-only `initialize`, and pair repair on failure. These match the pinned source.
- `initialize` needs revision 0 and writes no `.bak`. This is consistent with "Initialize receipt and revision 0 together".
- The `.lock` file is created lazily with `O_CREAT`. The gitignore-first ordering therefore covers it.

**Protocol envelope and receipts** (`Protocol`)
- The closed envelope and response, the closed error-code set, and "only `revision_conflict` retryable" are coherent.
- Reserved operations return `missing_capability` and never write.
- Rejections never create receipts.
- Lease-epoch revocation applies to new requests, while historical retrieval stays receipt-only. That separation is sound.

**Actors and lease** (`Actors and lease`)
- Epoch increases on acquire, recover and release, and the operator is rotated every time.
- The crash matrix covers stage, intent, credential publication, state publication and cleanup.
- Reconciliation is limited to the matching request, and a changed bootstrap request ID cannot reconcile another bootstrap.

**Export** (`Export and installed evidence`)
- The bundle has a closed key set and the exporter does not read live state.

**Cardinal-sin sweep**
- Rollback: package rollback plus the store backup and archive exit exist, subject to the blocker above.
- Migration: v1 refuses unsupported durable versions, no live state is migrated, and future expansion is owned by later PRDs.
- Write-path SPOF: explicitly accepted, with Bob named as operator.
- Observability: error codes, snapshot, and the `recovery_required` signal. Local-CLI-adequate.
- Resource bounds: sizes, depth and digits are bounded, and the CLI is bounded by caller deadlines.
- Deletion and export: handled by the never-delete archive and snapshot.
- Versioning: `protocol_major` plus the contract bundle.
- Secrets: gitignored, 0600, digest-only in state, and absent from responses and snapshot.
